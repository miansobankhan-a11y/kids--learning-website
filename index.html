Head

Top of the Body

💡 What does it do?

The head holds your brain, eyes, ears, nose, and mouth! It sits at the top of your neck and keeps your thinking organ safe inside a strong skull.

✨ Fun Fact for Class 3!

Your brain inside your head sends messages faster than a speedy sports car!

🎯 Try This Right Now!

Gently touch the top of your head and nod 3 times to say hello!

Our 5 Magic Senses 🪄

Our sense organs help us explore, understand, and enjoy the wonderful world around us!

👀

Eyes

Sense of Sight

Your eyes let you see everything from a tiny butterfly to tall mountains, bright colors, and your friends' smiles!

💡 Tip: Blink often and rest your eyes when reading or looking at a screen!
👂

Ears

Sense of Hearing

Ears catch sound waves so you can enjoy your favorite songs, listen to teacher stories, and hear warning horns.

💡 Tip: Never put sharp objects or pencils inside your ears!
👃

Nose

Sense of Smell

Your nose smells yummy baking cookies and sweet flowers. It also warms the air when you breathe in!

💡 Tip: Your nose can remember thousands of different smells!
👅

Tongue

Sense of Taste

Tiny taste buds on your tongue help you taste sweet mangoes, salty popcorn, and sour lemons. It also helps you talk!

💡 Tip: Clean your tongue gently with a toothbrush every day.
✋

Skin

Sense of Touch

Skin is the biggest organ of your entire body! It lets you feel a fluffy blanket, cold ice cubes, and warm sunshine.

💡 Tip: Wash your hands with soap to keep germs away from your skin!

Inside My Body: The Super Organs! 🫀

Even though we can't see them, these special organs work 24/7 like super-heroes to keep us alive and healthy!

🧠

The Brain

The Master Computer

Protected inside your skull, the brain tells your hands to write, remembers your friends' names, and helps you learn math!

⭐ Super Fact: When you sleep, your brain organizes memories like a tidy library!
❤️

The Heart

The Blood Pump

Your heart is about the size of your clenched fist. It goes thump-thump without resting, sending blood everywhere!

⭐ Super Fact: Your heart beats about 100,000 times in a single day!
🫁

The Lungs

The Air Balloons

Located in your chest, your two lungs expand like balloons when you inhale fresh oxygen, and shrink when you blow out!

⭐ Super Fact: Taking deep breaths helps you feel calm and happy!
🥣

The Stomach

The Food Blender

After you swallow food, it lands in your stomach. Special juices churn the food into liquid so your body gets power to play!

⭐ Super Fact: Chew food slowly to help your stomach digest easily!
🦴

Bones & Skeleton

The Strong Frame

Without bones, you would be as wobbly as a bowl of jelly! Bones give you shape, support you, and let you stand tall.

⭐ Super Fact: Milk, cheese, and sunshine help make your bones rock solid!
💪

Muscles

The Power Movers

You have over 600 muscles! Whenever you smile, jump, run, or write your name, muscles are pulling your bones to move.

⭐ Super Fact: It takes 43 muscles to frown, but only 17 to smile! So keep smiling!
