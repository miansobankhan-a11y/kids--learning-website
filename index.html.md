## 🚀 Live Demo & Features
- 🚶‍♂️ **Interactive Body Diagram:** Clickable SVG character with numbered hotspots from Head to Feet.
- 🔊 **Voice Narration (Read Aloud):** Built-in text-to-speech pronunciation and fun facts for young learners.
- 👀 **The 5 Senses:** Visual cards explaining Sight, Hearing, Smell, Taste, and Touch.
- 🫀 **Inside My Body (Super Organs):** Grade 3 explanations for Brain, Heart, Lungs, Stomach, Bones, and Muscles.
- 🎮 **Interactive Quiz ("Who Am I?"):** Multiple-choice questions with instant scoring and encouragement.
- 🍎 **Healthy Habits Checklist:** Interactive daily tracker to earn a Golden Health Medal.

## 🌐 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Free)
1. In your GitHub repository, click on **Settings** (⚙️).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment > Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)`.
4. Click **Save**. Within 1–2 minutes, your website will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<your-repo-name>/`!
